# Entity Fact Pack: Sole Prop vs General Partnership vs Stock Corp (incl. OPC)

Context: 3 founders (CEO, UI/UX lead, lead dev), zero cash to inject, pre-revenue laundry pickup/delivery, Zamboanga City. Register Nov 2026, closed pilot Dec 2026. AZUL Hub (DOST-AdZU TBI) and one angel in the picture.
Compiled 2026-10-27 from web sources. Confidence: H = official/primary or multiple consistent sources; M = reputable secondary; L = single or conflicting secondary. "Date" = source publish/review date where visible, else "undated".

## 1. Registration path and lead time (2026)

**Sole proprietorship (DTI BNRS)**
- Online at bnrs.dti.gov.ph; any Filipino 18+ may register; certificate downloadable after payment using the reference code and emailed verification code. Same-day in practice once paid; DTI does not publish an SLA. https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq (undated, footer 2016-2026) H on process, M on "same day".
- Pay within 7 calendar days of application or the application lapses. https://www.tripleiconsulting.com/dti-online-registration-practical-guide-for-business-name-registration/ (undated) M.
- Validity 5 years. https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq H.

**General partnership (SEC, Regular Processing on eSPARC)**
- Filed via eSPARC (esparc.sec.gov.ph); partnerships are NOT eligible for OneSEC, they go through Regular Processing. Online review "a few working days to about seven working days"; hard copies due within 60 calendar days of online approval; realistic 1 to 3 weeks end to end. https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/how-to-register-a-partnership-with-sec-philippines (2026-06-13) M; https://www.tripleiconsulting.com/?p=7985 (2026) M.
- Documents: Cover Sheet, name reservation confirmation, Articles of Partnership (notarized), Joint Undertaking to Change Name, IDs/TINs of partners. https://appointment.sec.gov.ph/primary-registration-2/processing-of-applications-for-partnerships (undated SEC page, fetched via search summary) H.
- Civil Code Art. 1772: partnership with capital of P3,000 or more (money or property) must be in a public instrument and recorded with SEC; Art. 1773: immovable contributions need an inventory. Respicio (2026-06-13) H (statute).

**Stock corporation (SEC eSPARC / OneSEC)**
- OneSEC = one-day e-registration for qualifying domestic stock corporations, 2 to 15 incorporators (also OPCs). Eligibility: 100% Filipino-owned (foreign-equity firms added Sept 2025), ordinary domestic stock corp, no secondary license, incorporators/directors/subscribers natural persons and PH residents, perpetual term, outside ecozone, common shares par >= P1, shares subscribed in cash, name carries an industry descriptor, primary purpose pre-filled from one of the permitted industry classes (expanded 33 -> 81 in 2025). Interim digital Certificate of Incorporation issued online; signed/notarized hard copies may be submitted within one year. https://filepino.com/onesec-application-process/ (updated 2026-08-14) M; https://www.aureadalaw.com/post/sec-expands-one-day-company-registration-to-81-industries (2025-09) M; https://malaya.com.ph/business/corporate/sec-eases-one-day-filing-rules-to-include-foreign-firms (2025-09-12) M.
- Regular eSPARC processing: 3 to 7 business days typical; 7 to 15 business days if special clearances or hard copies. https://wise.com/ph/blog/sec-business-registration (2025-11-19) M; Triple i 2026 guide M.
- SEC MC 3 s.2025 made the SEC ZERO platform mandatory for eSPARC/OneSEC filings. https://ndvlaw.com/tag/file-with-sec/ (2025) L.
- Whether "laundry services" is one of the 81 OneSEC industry classes is NOT confirmed (see Gaps). If not, falls to Regular Processing.

**Common post-registration steps (all forms)**
- Barangay clearance, then Mayor's/business permit (Zamboanga specifics in Sec. 9). BIR no longer requires the Mayor's permit to register a new business (RMC 91-2024). https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/insights/articles-and-updates1/tax-notes/clarifications-on-the-registration-procedures-under-eopt-act/ (2024) H.
- BIR: Form 1901 (sole prop / self-employed) or 1903 (corps, partnerships); register electronically (ORUS, orus.bir.gov.ph; business registration live again since 2024-10-10 per RMC 113-2024) or at the RDO; output = Form 2303 Certificate of Registration; register books of accounts; secure Authority to Print (ATP) invoices (or use e-invoicing/CAS). Deadline: on or before commencement of business = first sale or 30 days after DTI/SEC/LGU registration, whichever is first. https://batasnatin.com/laws/rmc-no-91-2024-clarification-on-registration-procedures-pursuant-to-revenue-r (2024) H; https://juan.tax/feed/ (forms refresh Oct 2025) L.
- Invoices replaced Official Receipts as the primary document for services from 2024-04-27 (RR 7-2024, as amended by RR 11-2024; RMC 77-2024). Non-VAT sellers must issue an invoice for any single sale above P500 or on request. https://pwc.com/ph/en/tax/tax-publications/taxwise-or-otherwise/2024/eopt-invoicing-clarified.html (2024) H.
- P500 BIR annual registration fee abolished by EOPT Act (RA 11976), effective 2024-01-22; Form 0605 no longer filed for it. https://kpmg.com/ph/en/home/insights/2024/01/ease-of-paying-taxes-act.html (2024-01) H; https://firstcircle.ph/blog/ease-of-paying-taxes-act (2024) H.
- BIR in-person processing typically 3 to 5 business days. https://firstcircle.ph/blog/bir-registration-process (undated) M.

## 2. Capital rules

- RCC (RA 11232) Sec. 12: no minimum capital stock for stock corporations unless a special law says otherwise; the old 25% subscribed / 25% paid / P5,000 minimum paid-up rule at incorporation is gone. The 25/25 rule survives only for INCREASES of capital stock. https://appointment.sec.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/2019RCC_Booklet.pdf (2019) H; https://www.pwc.com/ph/en/tax/tax-publications/taxwise-or-otherwise/2019-taxwise-or-otherwise/decoding-the-revised-corporation-code-part-1.html (2019) H. Note: Wise (2025-11-19) still repeats the 25/25 and P5,000 rule; treat as outdated. L.
- Practical floor: filing fee minimum P2,000 covers authorized capital up to P1,000,000 (1/5 of 1% of 1M = 2,000), so people commonly set authorized capital at P100,000 to P1,000,000 with small subscribed/paid-up amounts. OneSEC requires shares subscribed in cash and par >= P1, and does not state a minimum paid-up. Filepino (2026-08-14) M; https://www.tripleiconsulting.com/?p=8126 (2025) M. Exact "can you incorporate with P5,000 or P1,000 paid-up": legally yes under Sec. 12 (no floor), but no source found testing eSPARC with sub-P5,000 paid-up. L.
- Foreign-owned (>40%) domestic-market corps need USD 200,000 paid-up (FIA); irrelevant for all-Filipino founders. https://www.tripleiconsulting.com/minimum-paid-up-capital-guide-for-foreign-owned-corporations-philippines/ H.
- Partnership: no statutory minimum capital; P3,000 is the public-instrument/SEC recording threshold, not a floor. Capital may be money, property, or industry (labor/skill) via industrial partners. Civil Code Arts. 1767, 1772 (Respicio 2026-06-13) H.
- Sole prop: no capital requirement; DTI asks nothing about capital. https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq H.
- "No capital" practically: sole prop = P230 government money; partnership = founders may contribute industry only but SEC filing fee minimum P2,000 is still based on declared capital (a nominal cash capital, e.g. P3,000 to P10,000, is what practitioners typically declare); corporation = founders must subscribe and pay some cash (no floor) plus ~P3,000 to P4,000 SEC fees plus DST. M.

## 3. Government fees (2026)

**DTI (sole prop)**: Barangay P200, City/Municipality P500, Regional P1,000, National P2,000, each plus P30 DST. Late renewal +50%. https://bnrs.dti.gov.ph/faq H. A city-scope Zamboanga registration = P530 all-in.

**SEC (stock corporation)**
- Filing fee: 1/5 of 1% of authorized capital stock or subscription price of subscribed capital, whichever higher, minimum P2,000. Legal research fee 1% of filing fee, min P10. https://appointment.sec.gov.ph/?p=108 (SEC Registration Calculator, undated) H.
- Bylaws P1,010 (NDV, SEC calculator-aligned) vs P510 (Triple i 2025-11-30): conflicting. Stock & Transfer Book ~P470. Name reservation P100 per name (30 days) per Kittelson & Carpo; Wise says P120. eSECURE verification P400 (Wise). https://ndvlaw.com/how-to-form-a-corporation-in-the-philippines/ (2020-10-29) M; https://www.tripleiconsulting.com/hidden-fees-business-registration-philippines-nobody-tells-you-about/ (2025-11-30) M; https://wise.com/ph/blog/sec-business-registration (2025-11-19) L.
- CONFLICT to resolve: Wise (fees "as of 2025-10-24") lists filing fee as 0.01% of authorized capital, min P200; Aurea Law (2025-09) cites SEC MC 8 s.2025 giving a 20% registration-fee discount (up to 50% for capital-raising companies). The official calculator still shows 1/5 of 1% min P2,000. Budget P2,000; expect possibly less. L.
- Worked estimate, P100,000 to P1,000,000 authorized: P2,000 filing + P20 LRF + P510 to P1,010 bylaws + P470 STB + P100 name = roughly P3,100 to P3,600 SEC-side. M.
- DST on original issue of shares: P2.00 per P200 of par value (TRAIN, RR 4-2018), i.e. 1% of subscribed par. Due within 5 days after the close of the month of SEC registration, via BIR Form 2000 (RMC 56-2019). https://dev.lawyerly.ph/laws/89753 (RR 4-2018) H; https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/insights/articles-and-updates1/tax-notes/dst-on-original-issue-of-sharesof-stocks-for-new-corporations/ (2019-06-04) H. Example: P25,000 subscribed = P250 DST. (Respicio claims CREATE cut it to P1.50; unverified, treat P2.00 as current. L on the CREATE claim.)

**SEC (partnership)**: filing fee 1/5 of 1% of partnership capital, min P2,000, plus 1% legal research fee; name reservation ~P100; notarization of Articles. Practitioner all-in estimate P5,000 to P12,000 including notarial/professional costs. https://appointment.sec.gov.ph/primary-registration-2/processing-of-applications-for-partnerships (SEC, undated) H; Respicio (2026-06-13) M. No DST on partnership interests at formation (DST Sec. 174 covers shares of stock only). M.

**OPC**: same filing-fee formula as a stock corp (1/5 of 1%, min P2,000) plus bylaws not required (OPC has no bylaws) but nominee/alternate nominee consents and Form of Appointment of Officers are filed. https://kittelsoncarpo.com/how-to-register-an-opc-in-the-philippines/ (undated) M; https://pnl-law.com/blog/how-to-register-a-one-person-corporation-requirements-and-application-process/ M.

**LGU (any form)**: Mayor's permit fee is a fixed/bracketed fee; local business tax for a NEW business is assessed on declared capital investment (no prior gross receipts), renewals on prior-year gross receipts (LGC Sec. 143). Generic ranges: new-business tax 1/20 of 1% to a few tenths of a percent of capital; Mayor's permit P500 to P10,000+. https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/how-to-compute-mayors-permit-fees-and-local-business-taxes-in-the-philippines (undated) M. Zamboanga figures in Sec. 9.

**BIR (any form)**: P30 loose DST on the Certificate of Registration; books of accounts stamping free to ~P500 (physical) ; ATP/printing of invoices P2,000 to P4,000 from an accredited printer. Triple i (2025-11-30) M.

**All-in cash estimates (government + notarial, no lawyers)**
- Sole prop (city scope): DTI P530 + barangay ~P200 to 500 + Mayor's permit/fees (Zamboanga, see Sec. 9) ~P2,000 to 4,000 + BIR P30 + invoices ~P2,000 => roughly P5,000 to P8,000. M.
- Partnership: SEC ~P2,100 to 2,500 + notarization P500 to 2,000 + LGU/BIR as above => roughly P7,000 to P12,000. M.
- Stock corp: SEC ~P3,100 to 3,600 + DST on shares (P250 on P25k subscribed) + notarization P1,500 to 3,000 + LGU/BIR => roughly P9,000 to P15,000 DIY. Triple i's P85,000 to P150,000 figure includes professional fees and a P1M capital base; ignore for DIY. M.

## 4. Tax treatment (2026)

**Sole proprietor (individual)**
- Graduated rates: 0% up to P250,000; 15% to 35% above (TRAIN brackets effective 2023). Plus 3% percentage tax (2551Q) if non-VAT. Alternatively, if gross sales <= P3,000,000 (VAT threshold), elect 8% on gross sales above P250,000 in lieu of graduated income tax AND percentage tax; elect on first quarterly return. https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/insights/articles-and-updates1/tax-notes/availing-yourself-of-8-tax-rate-for-self-employed-individuals/ H; https://wise.com/ph/blog/how-much-tax-small-business-philippines (2025) M.
- VAT registration mandatory above P3,000,000 gross sales (12%). H.
- Pilot year effect: if 2026/2027 revenue is tiny, income tax under 8% option on a pre-revenue pilot is ~0 (first P250k exempt). H.
- Filings: 1701Q quarterly (3), 1701A or 1701 annual (April 15), 2551Q quarterly if not on 8%, plus withholding returns if any payroll. Audited FS only if gross sales/receipts > P3,000,000 (NIRC Sec. 232 as amended by TRAIN). https://www.filepino.com/who-is-required-to-submit-an-audited-financial-statement-afs/ M; Forvis Mazars tax calendars (2025-2026) M.
- EOPT taxpayer classes: Micro < P3M gross sales (lighter penalties, simpler rules). Grant Thornton (2024) H.

**General partnership (non-professional)**
- Taxed as a corporation: NIRC Sec. 22(B) "corporation" includes partnerships "no matter how created or organized" except general professional partnerships. https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html (NIRC text) H. A laundry business is NOT a GPP (GPP = common profession only, no trade/business). H.
- So: 20% or 25% RCIT (same tests as corporations), 2% MCIT from 4th year, files 1702Q/1702-RT. H.
- Partners' share in distributable net income after tax is taxed as dividends: 10% final tax (NIRC Sec. 24(B)(2), explicit "share of an individual in the distributable net income after tax of a partnership (except a GPP)"); Sec. 73(D) deems partnership net income constructively received by partners whether distributed or not. lawphil NIRC H for 24(B)(2); Sec. 73(D) from training knowledge, M.
- Net effect: double layer (20% entity + 10% on distributions) = ~28% combined on profits, versus 0% to 8% for a tiny sole prop. H.
- No SEC GIS for partnerships; AFS filing with SEC applies to partnerships too (SRC Rule 68 covers "corporations" but SEC requires partnership FS as well; threshold below). M.

**Stock corporation / OPC**
- RCIT 25%; 20% if net taxable income <= P5,000,000 AND total assets <= P100,000,000 (excluding land of office/plant). CREATE (RA 11534) rule, unchanged by CREATE MORE (RA 12066; CREATE MORE's 20% is for registered business enterprises under the enhanced deductions regime). https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/philippines/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income (reviewed 2026-08-01) H; https://chambers.com/articles/more-to-create (2024-2025) M.
- MCIT 2% of gross income from the 4th taxable year after start of operations (the 1% MCIT ran only 2020-07-01 to 2023-06-30). PwC (2026-08-01) H; Grant Thornton H.
- Dividends to resident individual stockholders: 10% final withholding tax (NIRC Sec. 24(B)(2)). lawphil H; https://batasnatin.com/laws/tax-dividends-Sec-32-A-7-cash-property-stock M.
- Inter-corporate dividends (domestic to domestic): exempt. PwC H.
- Filings: 1702Q (3 quarters), 1702-RT annual (April 15 for calendar year), 2551Q if non-VAT, withholding returns; SEC General Information Sheet within 30 calendar days after the annual stockholders' meeting (RCC Sec. 177); SEC AFS filing per SEC schedule (MC 6-2022 staggered by last digit of SEC number, ~May-July) or 120 days after FYE. https://batasnatin.com/laws/annual-sec-filings-general-information-sheet-gis-and-audited-financial-statement M; https://www.dfdl.com/insights/legal-and-tax-updates/philippines-schedule-and-procedure-for-the-filing-of-annual-financial-statements-general-information-sheet-other-annual-reports/ M.
- NEW: SEC MC 4 s.2026 (2026-01-20) raised the SEC audit threshold from P600,000 to P3,000,000 total assets OR total liabilities. Corps at or under that file unaudited FS with a sworn Statement of Management's Responsibility, for fiscal years ending on or after 2025-12-31. https://ndvlaw.com/the-secs-new-2026-afs-threshold/ (2026) M; https://tribune.net.ph/2026/01/21/small-companies-get-audit-relief (2026-01-21) M; https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/technical-alerts/accounting-alert/2025/amendments-to-the-application-of-audit-thresholds-under-revised-src-rule-68/ M. Boundary wording ("at or below" vs "less than") unconfirmed. L on boundary.
- BIR side: audited FS still required for any taxpayer with gross annual sales > P3,000,000 (Sec. 232). H.
- Compliance burden ranking: sole prop (1701Q x3, 1701A, 2551Q x4 or none on 8%) < partnership (1702Q x3, 1702-RT, 2551Q, SEC FS) < corporation (same as partnership + GIS + annual stockholders' meeting + board minutes + stock & transfer book + officer appointments). H.
- Late SEC filings: penalties P1,000 to P10,000+ per form per month. Wise (2025) L.

## 5. Liability

- Sole prop: no separate personality; owner personally liable for all business debts and torts, unlimited. Standard; H.
- General partnership: partnership is a juridical person (Civil Code Art. 1768), but Art. 1816: "All partners, including industrial ones, shall be liable pro rata with all their property and after all the partnership assets have been exhausted" for partnership contracts. Liability is joint (pro rata by headcount, not by capital share) and subsidiary (partnership assets first). Island Sales v. Daco (G.R. L-22493, 1975): each of 5 partners liable for 1/5. Guy v. Gacott (G.R. 206147, 2016) restates joint + subsidiary. Art. 1817: any stipulation against this is void as to third parties. https://jur.ph/jurisprudence/summary/island-sales-inc-v-daco H; https://ndvlaw.com/how-do-general-and-limited-partners-handle-debt/ M. Note: for TORTS/quasi-delicts (e.g. rider injures a pedestrian), Arts. 1822-1824 make partners SOLIDARILY liable with the partnership. H (statute).
- Stock corporation / OPC: stockholders' liability limited to unpaid subscription; corporate veil only pierced for fraud/alter ego. Officers may still be personally liable for their own negligence, unpaid SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG, and certain tax/labor violations. RCC; H.
- Road risk: Civil Code Art. 2180 makes an employer vicariously liable for employees' negligence within assigned tasks unless it proves diligence in selection and supervision; Art. 2184 for vehicle owners present in the vehicle. Whether "independent contractor" riders escape Art. 2180 turns on the control test (Nieves-Quinio v. Munoz; De Belen v. Fuchs, G.R. 258557, 2023). In a sole prop or general partnership this exposure lands on the founders' personal assets; in a corporation it stops at corporate assets absent fraud. https://lawphil.net/judjuris/juri2023/oct2023/gr_258557_2023.html (2023) H; https://tribune.net.ph/2026/10/19/vicarious-liability-2 (2026-10-19) M.
- Custody of customers' clothes: contract of deposit/bailment liability; same personal-vs-corporate split. M.
- Data Privacy Act: NPC Circular 2022-01 administrative fines 0.5% to 3% of annual gross income (grave) or 0.25% to 2% (major), capped at P5,000,000 per the adopted circular; criminal penalties under RA 10173 fall on responsible officers regardless of form. https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/data-technology/philippines-administrative-fines-for-data-privacy-infractions-to-be-imposed-starting-27-august-2022_1 (2022) H; https://iapp.org/news/b/philippines-npc-implements-cap-on-penalties-for-violations-of-the-data-privacy-act M.

## 6. Equity and fundraising

- Sole prop: exactly one owner; no way to give co-founders ownership, only contracts/profit-share agreements (which do not bind third parties and create tax/labor ambiguity). H.
- Partnership: ownership = partnership interest set in the Articles; industrial partners may hold a share of profits without cash. But adding an investor means amending the Articles with SEC, and admission of a new partner technically dissolves and re-forms the partnership (Civil Code Arts. 1828-1830); new partner is liable for pre-admission debts only up to partnership property (Art. 1826). No preferred interests, no SAFE/convertible-to-shares. https://ndvlaw.com/is-a-newly-admitted-partner-liable-for-old-debts/ M.
- Corporation: shares; multiple classes (common, preferred with dividend/liquidation preference, founders' shares with special rights for a limited period, redeemable, treasury) under RCC; vesting has no specific PH rule and is done via founders agreements, deeds of assignment, buyback options. SAFE or convertible notes are contracts that must either be registered with SEC or fit an exemption (one-on-one private placement with a single angel generally fits Sec. 10.1 exemptions). Investors and VCs default to a domestic stock corporation because preference, anti-dilution, drag-along require it. https://www.digest.ph/blog/startup-guide-vesting-shares-of-stock M; https://ndvlaw.com/issuing-preferred-or-non-voting-shares-in-the-philippines/ M; https://globallawexperts.com/subscription-agreements-philippines/ M; https://www.tripleiconsulting.com/?p=7476 L.
- OPC: single stockholder only; the moment shares go to a second holder, a notice of conversion to ordinary stock corp must be filed within 60 days (SEC MC 27 s.2020, RCC Secs. 131-132), with filing fees + DST. https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/insights/articles-and-updates1/tax-notes/guidelines-for-the-conversion-of-corporations-either-to-one-person-corporation-or-to-ordinary-stock-corporation/ (2020) H.
- Conversion cost, sole prop -> corp: there is no legal conversion; you incorporate a new entity (full SEC fees + DST), get a NEW TIN and 2303, new books/ATP/invoices, new Mayor's permit, transfer assets/contracts (possible VAT/DST/CGT on transfers of property), then close the sole prop with BIR (tax clearance, P500 to 2,000) and cancel DTI. Several weeks. Same pattern partnership -> corp (dissolve partnership with SEC and BIR). https://www.tripleiconsulting.com/?p=9075 (2025) M; https://www.respicio.ph/commentaries/how-to-convert-a-sole-proprietorship-to-a-one-person-corporation-in-the-philippines M.
- Incubator/government norms: RA 11337 (Innovative Startup Act) defines a startup as "any person or registered entity" and a startup enabler must be a "registered entity"; benefits include subsidies for registration costs and expedited processing. DOST TBIs (AZUL Hub is a DOST-PCIEERD TBI launched 2023-02-27) list "business registration" as an incubation service, implying pre-registration entry is normal; no published AZUL eligibility rule requiring SEC registration found. DOST SETUP (grants/loans) requires 3+ years operation, Filipino ownership, business registration certificates (DTI/SEC/CDA) and permits: not a pilot-stage fit. QBO/IdeaSpace: no SEC-registration precondition found; incorporation assistance offered. https://ldr.senate.gov.ph/sites/default/files/2021-08/20190717-RA-11337-RRD.pdf (2019) H; https://mirror.pia.gov.ph/news/2023/02/28/dost-adzu-opens-azul-hub-in-zambo-city (2023-02-28) H; https://jur.ph/law/facts/small-enterprise-technology-upgrading-program-setup-guidelines (2015/2016 guidelines) M; https://www.dti.gov.ph/archives/news-archives/qbo-launches-hub-for-startup-programs/ M.

## 7. Payment providers and banks

- PayMongo accepts sole props (DTI cert + owner ID + 2303), partnerships (SEC Cert of Partnership + Articles + partners' resolution + partner ID + 2303), corporations (SEC COI + AOI/bylaws + latest GIS + rep ID + 2303 + notarized Secretary's Certificate), OPCs (COI + AOI + Form of Appointment of Officers + 2303 + owner ID + Secretary's Cert). Activation up to 14 business days. Note: a brand-new corp has no GIS yet; PayMongo's list says "latest GIS received by SEC", so a fresh corp may need to file its first GIS or get an exception. https://docs.paymongo.com/docs/account-settings-philippine-entities (updated 2026-05-07) H; https://developers.paymongo.com/docs/account-activation M.
- GCash for Business: accepts sole prop (DTI + 2303), partnership (+ SEC + Articles of Partnership), corporation (+ SEC + AOI + latest GIS), with board/partnership resolution and signatory IDs. HARD GATE: applicant's personal GCash must be fully verified with at least 12 months of activity; low-risk industry; one account per company. https://help.gcash.com/hc/en-us/articles/48456974006041 (current) H; https://gcash.com/business/msme H.
- Maya Business: sole prop (DTI cert, notarized SPA for signatories), partnership (latest GIS + notarized partners' resolution), corporation (latest GIS + notarized board resolution); KYC selfie + ID. Initial merchant onboarding document list not published; deposit account only for existing Maya Business merchants. https://www.maya.ph/business/stories/how-to-open-a-business-deposit-or-savings-account (2023-07-17) M.
- Banks (BDO merchant/business pages): sole prop = DTI registration; partnership = Articles of Partnership + SEC cert + notarized partnership resolution; corporation = latest GIS or AOI + SEC cert if newly registered + notarized board resolution/secretary's certificate. Typical corporate account minimum opening deposits P50,000 to P100,000 at major banks (practitioner claim); sole-prop business accounts open on personal-account minimums. https://www.bdo.com.ph/minipos/application-requirements H (documents); https://movetoasia.com/en/philippines/business/banking/ L (deposit minimums). Corporate account opening is a known launch delay (weeks). https://www.tripleiconsulting.com/?p=8800 M.
- Bottom line: all three forms are accepted by PayMongo, GCash, Maya, and banks; sole prop has the shortest document chain; corporation/partnership need a GIS or fresh SEC papers plus notarized resolutions. H.

## 8. Special forms

- OPC: single natural-person stockholder who is sole director and president; must name a nominee and alternate nominee (written consents filed); appoint corporate secretary (Filipino resident, not the owner) and treasurer within 15 days of COI and report within 5 days; no bylaws; no minimum capital; same 20%/25% CIT; limited liability. Pros for a founder-led start: fastest corporate form with limited liability, OneSEC-eligible. Cons here: only ONE owner, so the other two founders get nothing until conversion (mandatory within 60 days of a share transfer); conversion = amended AOI + bylaws + fees. Not a fit for 3 co-founders unless two are content to be employees/contractors for now. https://kittelsoncarpo.com/how-to-register-an-opc-in-the-philippines/ M; https://pnl-law.com/blog/how-to-register-a-one-person-corporation-requirements-and-application-process/ M; Grant Thornton (2020) H.
- Professional partnership (GPP): only for a common licensed profession; irrelevant to laundry. H.
- Limited partnership: needs at least one general partner (unlimited liability) plus limited partners (liable only to contribution, lose protection if they take part in control, Art. 1848); name must carry "Limited"/"Ltd."; filed as a certificate under Art. 1844 via eSPARC. Does not solve the founders' problem since all three would be active (control) and someone must be the general partner. https://www.filepino.com/limited-partnership-philippines/ M; https://chanrobles.com/legal5cc1c.htm (Civil Code text) H.
- Incorporators: RCC Sec. 10 allows 1 (OPC) or 2 to 15 incorporators, natural persons of legal age or juridical persons, each holding at least one share; no residency majority rule any more (OneSEC separately requires residents). https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/insights/articles-and-updates1/tax-notes/guidelines-on-the-number-and-qualifications-of-incorporators-under-the-revised-corporation-code/ (2019) H.
