# Zamboanga laundry survey script (week 1, 20 shops by phone, 5 visited)

Ask the owner or manager. Same order every time. Write the answer before moving on. Both frames to every shop.

| # | Question | Record |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What is your wash-dry-fold price per kg for walk-ins? Any minimum kg or minimum charge? | ₱/kg, min |
| 2 | How long for a 6 kg bag on a normal day? | hours |
| 3 | Roughly how many hours a day are the machines idle? Busiest and quietest days? | hours, days |
| 4 | Frame A. If we bring you 10 or more bags a week on a fixed schedule, pick up and return them ourselves, and pay you every week by GCash, what is the lowest price per kg you would take? | ₱/kg |
| 5 | Frame B. Would you instead list on an app at your own price, with 12% taken out of each order, paid weekly, our rider doing pickup and return? | yes / no / depends on |
| 6 | How many customers already ask you for pickup by Messenger or phone each week? | count |
| 7 | If a customer refuses the bag at the door, who should carry that cost? | shop / platform / split |
| 8 | Anything you would need from us to say yes? | free text |

Thresholds written down before the calls (founder judgement):
- Option 1 confirmed: 5 or more of 20 at or below ₱22/kg in Frame A (3 or more at ₱20), idle hours reported, dispersion narrow.
- Option 2 instead: fewer than 5 at ₱22, or 5 or more preferring Frame B over Frame A.

Shops to visit: Tetuan Laundry Hub (Don Alfaro St.), Guiwan Wash Center, Labada de Victoria, plus two more from the LaundryAtlas Zamboanga list (119 entries).
