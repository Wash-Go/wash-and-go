Rules that keep it honest

Do

  • Lead every order screen with who has the clothes, the weight, and the amount owed.
  • One stamp-colored button per screen. It is the next step for whoever is looking.
  • Put money in mono, large, and in the same place on list and detail.
  • Rider: primary action in a sticky bottom bar, ≥56px, amount in the slide label.
  • Show status as icon + word + phase color, with a plain-language label per audience.

Don't

  • No water: bubbles, aqua, sparkle icons, spin-cycle art.
  • No white text on #C96A28 or #D07A29. Use Stamp #A64B16.
  • No emoji in UI chrome; one icon set.
  • No invented stats, stars, or "partner shops" before real ones exist.
  • No raw server errors, URLs, or order codes as headlines.
