Partner laundry portal · UX preview

The shop's side of the claim ticket.

Every screen a partner laundry touches during a shift, drawn in the Claim Ticket system before any app code moves. Phone first, because the scale is next to the counter and the laptop usually isn't.

Sample shop: Tetuan Laundry Hub, Don Alfaro St., Tetuan, Zamboanga City. Rates: ₱25/kg wash, dry & fold, ₱40 delivery, ₱7 service fee. The shop keeps 88% of the wash value.

D. Portal rules

From DESIGN.md and the plan. Every screen above follows them; QA flags any build that doesn't.

  1. 44px targets, 48px buttonsWet hands, one thumb. Nothing tappable smaller than 44px; primary money actions on a 56px input.
  2. 16px inputsEvery input, select and textarea at 16px or more, so iOS never zooms the page.
  3. One primary action per cardOne navy button per card and per screen; everything else is secondary, text, or inside the detail.
  4. Tables become cards under 560pxBatches, lines and services read as cards on a phone; the desktop table keeps the same order of facts.
  5. Money, weights, codes and times in monoAtkinson Hyperlegible Mono, tabular, right-aligned in tables, in the same place on list and detail.
  6. Status is icon + word + colourLabels from packages/domain; phase colours, never a rainbow; "Needs you" overrides the phase for the shop.
  7. Human errors with a next stepNo server text. Every error says what happened and offers retry, back, or contact ops.
  8. Nothing destructive without a confirmRemoving staff and changing the payout account confirm first; heavy weigh-ins confirm twice.
